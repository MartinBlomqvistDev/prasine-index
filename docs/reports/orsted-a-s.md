### Regulatory Context

The Commission services' Questions and Answers (September 2026) say: "The prohibition in Annex I point 4c does not apply to claims based on offsetting at company level, however such claims remain subject to other relevant provisions of the UCPD."

Annex I point 4b [17] prohibits in all circumstances: "Making an environmental claim about the entire product or the trader's entire business when it concerns only a certain aspect of the product or a specific activity of the trader's business."
